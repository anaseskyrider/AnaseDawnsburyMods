# [More Archetypes](...)

Adds and remasters many Archetypes. See workshop page for content listing.

## Differences from Tabletop
- **Archer/Quick Shot.** This is Dawnsbury Days' Quick Draw.
- **Assassin/**
- - **Poison Resistance.** Rounds your level up (identical to the Druid feat in DD).
- - **Poison Weapon.** Reduces the costs of drawing and applying poisons, and adds an action for applying your simple poisons without fumbling with your inventory. The improved feat works as tabletop for the prepared poisons, and uses the Sticky Poison feat for all others.
- - **Assassinate.** You can be undetected instead of unnoticed.
- **Bastion/Nimble Shield Hand.** Because you can only have one item per hand, this allows you to Draw or Pick Up a shield, or replace an item with a shield, as a free action. Except for tower shields (or shields with the Cover Shield trait if using [⚙️More Shields](https.//steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3538575665) mod). This is slightly buffed from tabletop in some respects (such as picking up your shield after falling unconscious).
- **Familiar Master/Mutable Familiar.** Due to technical limitations, this changes your familiar preparation to precombat instead of morning, letting all abilities be changed each combat.
- **Continual Recovery.** Homebrewed to have an in-combat effect. If you critically succeed with Battle Medicine, the immunity is reduced to 1 encounter. At legendary in Medicine, this is reduced to 1 round.
- **Ward Medic.** Homebrewed to have an in-combat effect. If you have two free hands, you can use Battle Medicine and Treat Poison on more targets at once, scaling with Medicine proficiency.
